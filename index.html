    • About Me!

    I am an Enterprise & Technical Architect passionate about applying AI to solve real-world problems. Most of my time goes into building practical solutions with Claude Code and OpenAI, with a focus on helping financial institutions streamline operations, modernize legacy systems, and work more efficiently. I've been fortunate to witness the evolution of enterprise technology firsthand , from the mainframe era of IBM 4331, IBM 9375 and IBM 390 with COBOL and CICS, navigating green-screen 3270 terminals, through the rise of distributed thick-client architectures, and into SOAP APIs built on Java Spring, with application servers on IBM ESB and web servers on Apache. After 2000, I transitioned into building REST APIs, moving away from traditional data center server farms toward cloud-native architectures with Docker containers orchestrated by Kubernetes.

    Previously

Before that, in Sydney, I worked on Australia's first bank payment system, built on US-developed software, troubleshooting issues on US hours from Sydney.
